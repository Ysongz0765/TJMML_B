# TJMML_B Figure Palette

Use a white page and low-saturation fills. The canonical colors are:

| role | hex | use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mist blue | `#8DB3D0` | primary series, observed data |
| cream | `#E5DABC` | secondary series, uncertainty fill |
| sage | `#C0D7BE` | focal model or positive contrast |
| ice blue | `#DCE9ED` | background band, missingness, light panel |

Guidelines:

- Keep text and axes neutral gray rather than pure black.
- Use darker same-family tones for outlines when needed.
- Use alpha for uncertainty and secondary series.
- Do not use `jet`, `rainbow`, or default `tab10`.
- Do not use a color to imply significance unless the statistical rule is explicit.
